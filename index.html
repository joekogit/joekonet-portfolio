How I work

The hard part is never the code.It's getting a team of designers and engineers to agree on what a button is, then keeping that agreement alive for three roadmap cycles. Systems work is mostly diplomacy with a component library attached.

The underlying experience matters most.Component architecture, performance, optimization, accessibility. The stuff nobody notices until it's wrong. That's where I started and I never left.

One good agent is easy.The work is making hundreds of them coherent, governable, and worth trusting. That's what I spend my days on now.

Fewer repeated arguments.At Peacock the token rebuild cut design-to-dev translation time by about a third. Good number. What I cared about more was that teams stopped relitigating the same decisions every sprint.
